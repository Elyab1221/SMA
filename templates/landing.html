Key Principles

Why We Built SMA

A club thrives when ideas flow without hesitation or fear of judgment.

🛡️

True Anonymity

When you send a message, your student ID and email are never recorded in the message database. Admins only see the thought, never the sender.

👑

Leader & Admin Hierarchy

Club leaders create their hub, generate unique club join codes, and approve verified staff before admins can access incoming messages.

⚡

🔒

User Privacy & Security

Complete self-service password management for every student, automated brute-force protection, and strict CSRF defensive shielding.

Workflow

How Club SMA Operates

Three straightforward steps to transparent club communication.

01

Leader Creates Club

The club leader registers and receives an exclusive Club Code (e.g. SMA-7X9P2K) to distribute to their team and student body.

02

Admins Sign Up & Get Approved

Club staff sign up under the leader's code. For maximum security, admins remain pending until explicitly approved by the leader.

03

Students Speak Up Freely

Club members join using the code, compose feedback or questions, and submit directly to the inbox with total anonymity guaranteed.
